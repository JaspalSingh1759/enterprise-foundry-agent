# SOP: Unrecognised charges and sending a case to the fraud team

**Policy:** Card Dispute Policy 4.1. **Network reason code:** NS-10.4 (fraud team only).

## When to use

The customer says they did not make, or do not recognise, a charge.

## Steps

1. Look up the merchant behind the statement descriptor. Descriptors are often shortened or carry a payment processor's prefix (`SQ *`, `UBR*`, a phone number).
2. List the customer's history with that merchant.
3. **Genuine:** regular past purchases or a subscription paid in earlier months. Deny, and explain which merchant the descriptor belongs to.
4. **Signs of fraud:** card-not-present charges at foreign merchants never used before, several charges minutes apart, a small "test" charge before larger ones. Send the case to the fraud team. Do not propose a credit; the fraud team decides.
5. If unsure, send it to the fraud team. A missed fraud case is worse than an extra referral.

## What not to do

- Don't tell the customer the case is fraud. Say it has been passed to the specialist team.
- Don't block the card yourself; the fraud team does that.
