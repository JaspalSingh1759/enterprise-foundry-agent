# SOP: Privacy and personal information in disputes

## Rules

1. Use only the information needed to decide the case.
2. Refer to customers by case ID in notes, tickets and chat. Don't copy names or addresses into free-text fields.
3. Attachments stay attached to the case. Don't download them to personal devices or forward them by email.
4. Card numbers: last 4 digits only, everywhere.
5. Logs and monitoring tools must never contain claim text, names or document contents; IDs only.
6. A customer who asks what we hold about them is referred to the privacy office.
