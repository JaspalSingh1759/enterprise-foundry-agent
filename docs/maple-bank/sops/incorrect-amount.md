# SOP: Handling an incorrect amount

**Policy:** Card Dispute Policy 4.3 and 3.1. **Network reason code:** NS-12.2.

## When to use

The customer says they were charged more than the agreed price.

## Steps

1. Find the posted charge on the card.
2. Read the receipt, invoice or quote. No document, or unreadable: **request information**.
3. Compare the document's total (tax included) with the posted amount.
4. **Foreign currency:** if the receipt is in another currency, the posted CAD amount includes conversion and Maple Bank's 2.5% fee. If the posted amount matches the original amount converted plus the fee, it is not an error: deny and explain the conversion.
5. Otherwise recommend a credit of **posted amount minus the receipt total**.

## Common mistakes

- Using the receipt's subtotal instead of its total. Tax is part of the agreed price.
- Treating a currency conversion difference as an overcharge.
