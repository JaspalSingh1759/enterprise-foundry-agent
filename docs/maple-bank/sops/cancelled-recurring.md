# SOP: Handling a cancelled recurring charge

**Policy:** Card Dispute Policy 4.5 and 3.1. **Network reason code:** NS-13.7.

## When to use

The customer cancelled a subscription or membership and is still being charged.

## Steps

1. Read the cancellation confirmation and note the **cancellation date**. No confirmation: **request information**.
2. List the merchant's charges on the card.
3. Charges dated **after** the cancellation date are eligible. Charges on or before it stand.
4. If no charge is after the cancellation date, deny and explain.
5. Recommend a credit of the total of the eligible charges, listing each transaction ID.

## Common mistakes

- Crediting charges from before the cancellation.
- Accepting the customer's remembered date instead of the date on the confirmation.
