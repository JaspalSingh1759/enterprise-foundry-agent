# SOP: Customer communication

## Rules

1. Write in the customer's preferred language (English or French), using the approved templates in `templates/`.
2. Say what we decided, why, and what happens next, in plain words.
3. Quote amounts exactly, in CAD, with two decimals.
4. Never include the full card number; the last 4 digits are enough.
5. Never mention internal tools, scores or the fraud team's methods.
6. Requests for information say exactly what is needed and that the customer has **30 days** to send it.
