# SOP: Handling goods or services not received

**Policy:** Card Dispute Policy 4.6 and 3.1. **Network reason code:** NS-13.1.

## When to use

The customer paid and the goods or services never arrived.

## Steps

1. Read the order confirmation and note the **expected delivery date**.
2. If that date had not passed on the submission date: deny for now and tell the customer they can submit again after it.
3. Check the claim says the customer contacted the merchant. If not: **request information**, asking them to contact the merchant first and tell us the result.
4. Otherwise recommend a credit of the amount charged for the undelivered order.

## Common mistakes

- Accepting a claim before the expected delivery date.
- Skipping the merchant-contact requirement (the network requires it).
