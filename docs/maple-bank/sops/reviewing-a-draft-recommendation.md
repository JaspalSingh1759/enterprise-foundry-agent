# SOP: Reviewing a draft recommendation

Drafts are prepared by the dispute assistant. An analyst reviews every draft before anything happens.

## Checklist

1. **Facts:** the transactions cited are the ones the claim is about.
2. **Rules:** the time limit and approval threshold come from the rules tool, not from the draft's wording.
3. **Policy:** the sections cited support the outcome.
4. **Amount:** recompute it from the evidence. Any difference: reject the draft.
5. **Evidence:** open each attachment yourself for amounts above $250.

## Outcomes

- **Approve:** the draft is right as it stands.
- **Reject:** give a reason. Every rejection is reviewed and may become a test case for the assistant.
- **Request information:** evidence is missing.
