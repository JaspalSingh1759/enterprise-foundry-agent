# SOP: Approving high-value credits

**Senior analysts only.** **Policy:** Card Dispute Policy 3.3.

## When to use

A recommended credit is **more than $1,000.00**.

## Steps

1. Confirm you did not prepare or submit the case.
2. Complete the full review checklist (SOP: Reviewing a draft recommendation), opening every attachment.
3. Check the customer's past disputes: more than two credited disputes in 12 months needs a note on the case.
4. For credits over **$5,000.00**, add a second senior analyst's approval.
5. Record your decision and reason.
